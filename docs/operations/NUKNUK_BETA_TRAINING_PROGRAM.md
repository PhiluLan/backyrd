# NukNuk Beta training program

Status: **DRAFT — NOT AUTHORIZED FOR TRAINING OR ACTIVATION**.
Task class: FAST_PR (synthetic diagnostic data and export tooling only).
Depends on the Product V7 training foundations in PR #804. This does not change
an installed runtime, a model, production data or infrastructure.

## Goal and catalog

Teach accurate intent preservation and citation-bound dialogue updates. Evaluate
language understanding separately from candidate retrieval, eligibility, ranking
and visible explanations. Training cannot repair missing or incorrectly tagged
spot data.

Canonical source: `scripts/decision-diagnostics/nuknuk-beta-training-cases.mjs`.
The program and proposed resource/acceptance rules are generated by
`nuknuk-beta-training-program.mjs`. Extend the existing Product training exporter;
do not introduce another training or serving path.

There are 300 authored synthetic families, each in German, Swiss German, English
and French: 1,200 rows, split into 800 TRAIN, 200 VALIDATION and 200 TEST.
Four translations are correlated; these are not 1,200 independent situations.
Effective dialogue edits share a split even when their untouched prior activity
differs. Numeric-only variants and known historical patterns are kept together.
The historical 152-case bank is observed regression evidence, never a fresh test.
These structural rules do not replace a complete semantic equivalence review.

The curriculum covers concrete food/activity retention, relatives and recipients,
adult offspring, accompanied and unaccompanied minors, dietary subgroups,
budget boundaries and scopes, time scopes and references, optionality and
exclusions, alternatives, sequence, shared venues, fallback, observed and
conditional weather, initial booking requirements and dialogue corrections.
The supplemental specification contains 32 routing cases, 20 three-turn
conversations and 24 boundary cases. These are declared test specifications;
they have not been executed against a model and are not an executable oracle.

## Proposed controlled training and acceptance

Subject to the prerequisites and a separately authorized plan: continue the
verified current parent adapter, rank 16 / alpha 32, one epoch, microbatch 1,
accumulation 4, learning rate 6e-6 and seed 42. The 800 rows imply 200 optimizer
updates under those trainer semantics. Checkpoints are recovery only. Select on
DEV, freeze the exact checkpoint, then evaluate TEST once. Never truncate input.
The current limits are 5,120 total tokens and 2,048 answer tokens, subject to
preflight on the actual tokenizer/template and trainer configuration.

Require at least 190/200 fully correct test rows, at least 95% per language and
95% of authored test families correct in all four languages. **Every** test case
marked critical before splitting must pass; an arbitrary 80-row selection is
insufficient. Also report effective semantic-group results and all historical
material regressions. JSON validity alone is not semantic acceptance.

Full conversations, routing, instrumented boundary checks and native MLX
baseline/candidate comparison remain mandatory. Proposed p95 latency goals are
10 seconds simple / 15 seconds complex; these are goals, not measured results.
Phone and downstream catalog checks are required before Beta activation.
No model is activated or published as part of this program.

## Reproduction

Run the affected checks from the worktree:

```sh
node --test scripts/decision-diagnostics/test_nuknuk_beta_training.mjs scripts/decision-diagnostics/test_nuknuk_product_training.mjs
node scripts/decision-diagnostics/nuknuk-training-bundle.mjs --product-search-v7-beta OUTPUT MODEL_DIR CURRENT_MLX_ADAPTER CURRENT_HF_ADAPTER
```

OUTPUT must be a new directory under an existing parent. Export uses the real
provider prompt via an offline in-memory stub; no provider call is sent. Follow
with `nuknuk-curriculum/preflight.py` in the established tokenizer/grammar Python
environment. Keep exports, model files and review evidence under ignored local
operations, not in Git. The manifest binds source hashes, parent identities,
splits and payloads. Never overwrite an already reviewed export.
