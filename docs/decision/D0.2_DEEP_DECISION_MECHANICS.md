## Integrity-stop resolution

The dedicated D0.P0 repair adds `s.status = 'approved'::public.spot_status` to the `base_spots` candidate universe in `backyrd_get_decision_debug_v3`. This is the earliest shared scorer used by V11 and therefore protects V12, Mobile V13 personalized candidates, Public Web, and the developer Decision Debug path before ranking. No score formula, weight, ordering rule, or Distribution policy changed.

The other V13 sources were independently re-verified:

- `backyrd_match_spot_embeddings_v13` already requires `approved`;
- `distribution_trust_spot_catalog_v1`, including city and global fallback calls, already requires `approved`;
- V13 fusion only unions V12, semantic, and fallback candidates and does not create new Spot IDs.

The durable regression `supabase/tests/sprint_decision_product_eligibility.sql` passed on a fresh canonical database boot. It proves pending and rejected exact-name exclusion, broad-query exclusion, V11, authenticated isolated V12 and run-item persistence, Semantic V13, fallback, the complete V13 source union, approved-only order/score stability, repeated execution, and the Product Eligibility × Distribution matrix. Finding `D0-F-001` is resolved in code. Production remains unchanged until the forward migration is reviewed and deployed through the normal release process.

This resolution authorizes resumption of the remaining forensic D0.2 work on the corrected canonical branch. It does not make the unfinished D0.2 report pass and does not make D1 ready.
