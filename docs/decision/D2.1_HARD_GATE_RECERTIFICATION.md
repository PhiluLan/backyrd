# D2.1 Hard Gate Enforcement & Constitution Re-Certification

## Executive summary

D2 v1 declared more non-compensable quality gates than its evaluator enforced. D2.1 repairs the measuring instrument without changing V11, V12, V13, semantic retrieval, Product/Distribution policy, latent utility, soft thresholds or any Product surface.

The v1.1 Constitution, scenario contract, evaluator, result schema and acceptance suite are independently versioned and frozen. Nine hard gates are declared, registered, implemented and adversarially covered. The deterministic suite contains 45 cases: nine valid, nine single-violation, nine boundary, nine multi-violation and nine missing-evidence/malformed cases. False-pass, false-fail and `NOT_EVALUATED` leakage rates are all 0%.

## What was wrong with D2 v1

The JSON/prose Constitution declared approved Product state, eligible Distribution, entity integrity, latent-leakage freedom, must-pass constraints and zero duplicates. Golden scenarios structurally contained city, category, exclusions and open-now requirements. However:

1. `attributeFailure()` created hard records only for status and Distribution values copied from `trace.results`.
2. The aggregator defined `hardGates.pass` as the absence of P0/P1 failure records, so unenforced gates were invisible.
3. Scenario requirements affected parts of Ground Truth but were not checked against every returned result. City/open-now were not in the eligible universe at all.
4. Duplicate response was measured as a soft metric but not connected to the declared hard limit.
5. Acceptance proved isolated metrics reacted but never adversarially passed every declared violation through the evaluator.
6. Readiness checked that the existing suite exited green, not Constitution ↔ Registry ↔ Evaluator ↔ Tests completeness.

This allowed the D3 preflight counterexamples to return `engineQuality: PASS` and `hardGates.pass: true`.

## Complete hard-gate inventory

All gates inspect the complete returned result set. Candidate identity and attributes resolve from the synthetic World; trace claims cannot grant eligibility.

| Gate | Applicability | PASS | FAIL | NOT_EVALUATED / invalid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `PRODUCT_ELIGIBILITY` | Every scenario | All Spots are `approved` | Any known non-approved state | Canonical status missing |
| `DISTRIBUTION_ELIGIBILITY` | Every scenario | `normal` or `reduced` | `quarantined` or `excluded` | Missing/unknown state |
| `ENTITY_INTEGRITY` | Every scenario | Every ID resolves to a complete canonical synthetic Spot | Missing ID/entity or malformed entity | Malformed entity is treated as integrity FAIL, not engine evidence uncertainty |
| `LATENT_LEAKAGE` | Every scenario | No forbidden evaluation-truth key in request/trace | Forbidden key present | Request/trace evidence absent |
| `CITY` | A hard city is declared | Every Spot city equals the declaration | Any different city | Missing canonical city |
| `HARD_CATEGORY` | A hard category is declared | Exact canonical category match | Any different known category | Unknown declaration/missing category evidence |
| `CATEGORY_EXCLUSION` | Exclusion list is non-empty | No excluded category returned | Any excluded category returned | Unknown exclusion/missing category evidence |
| `OPEN_NOW` | `openNow = true` | Every Spot is open in the declared D2 time bucket | Any Spot is closed | Time bucket or Boolean opening truth missing |
| `DUPLICATE_RESULTS` | Every scenario | Spot-ID duplicate rate is exactly 0 | First duplicate (`rate > 0`) | Malformed/missing results contract is rejected before gate evaluation |

`NORMAL` and `REDUCED` are equally eligible; D0-F-002 remains a separate Distribution-priority defect. Empty results are hard-valid and may still be soft-quality poor.

No soft threshold or utility weight changed.

## New result contract

Each gate yields `PASS`, `FAIL`, `NOT_EVALUATED` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`, plus expected value, observed evidence, reason and result-level synthetic IDs/ranks where applicable. Scenario output aggregates applicable, passed, failed, not-evaluated and not-applicable counts.

- any applicable `FAIL` → hard status `FAIL`, `hardGates.pass = false`, Engine Quality cannot pass;
- no failure but any applicable `NOT_EVALUATED` → incomplete, non-certifiable, Engine Quality `NOT_EVALUATED`;
- all applicable gates `PASS` → hard status `PASS`;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE` never masquerades as missing evidence.

Certification and quality remain distinct. A hard-valid poor ranking is certifiable but may fail later soft-quality aggregation. A hard-invalid perfect ranking is not certifiable.

## Scenario contract

`golden-scenarios-v1.1` adds `applicableHardGates` and `hardGateScope: FULL_RETURNED_SET`. This is a structural expression of existing requirements, not a semantic change. All 42 scenarios, 18 families and split counts remain unchanged: 18 Development, 12 Regression, 12 Locked Holdout. No V13 result existed or was inspected when the structure was added.

## Remaining limitations

- D2 opening truth is a deterministic Boolean per time bucket. Exact clock-boundary, special-hours and cross-midnight semantics are not represented and are explicitly not calibrated; D2.1 does not invent them.
- Repository-visible Locked Holdout remains process-isolated/tamper-evident, not genuinely secret.
- Production latency, real outcomes and real satisfaction remain uncalibrated.
- D0-F-002 remains known and unfixed.

## Governance

A future hard gate requires, in one versioned change: Constitution declaration, Registry entry, applicability/evidence contract, evaluator, valid/single/boundary/multi/missing-evidence coverage and a new freeze. Removal or Hard ↔ Soft conversion is a Constitution semantic change requiring explicit product approval and a new freeze.
