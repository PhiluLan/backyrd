# N5.6.1 Validation Contract

Version: `backyrd-n5-6-1-validation-contract-v1`

The contract was frozen before the official sealed run. It compares the frozen N5.6 control projection with N5.6.1 on the identical N5.6 User Cards and world, plus two predeclared adversarial Moments: new-city Broad Unknown and Museum/Culture Family.

## Mandatory gates

The frozen gates cover scientific identity, complete Concept metadata, Current Intent authority, signed polarity, full auditability, zero controlled Context/Place-Type leakage, Global fallback control, cap-as-maximum, profile-dump control, Broad uncertainty, DEEP/unknown-context honesty, Solo retention, Family honesty, Cross-City portability, Cold/Developing calibration, same/different-user semantics, relevant negative retention, relevance precision/recall, suppression precision/recall, decision-specific sufficiency, intent duplication, independent signals, privacy, and zero AI calls.

Profile-dump limits were frozen at cap-hit rate ≤ `0.10`, mean selection ratio ≤ `0.20`, irrelevant-selected rate ≤ `0.05`, and same-family/same-polarity duplication rate ≤ `0.35`. Same-User semantic differentiation must exceed `0.28`, demonstrating improvement over the N5.6 result `0.266062` without requiring arbitrary maximum difference from a world with repeated concept bundles.

## Scientific boundaries

Stop conditions include incomplete metadata, unfrozen relevance/sufficiency, evaluator leakage, unresolved negative-preference failure, incomplete suppression audit, and post-freeze threshold changes.

Human Review is mandatory even on PASS. N6 is never automatically authorized.
