## 2. Boundary Finding

The original D4 layer order remains correct:

`Eligibility → Retrieval → Candidate Evidence → Contextual Personalized Utility → Fusion → Final Ranking`

The strict assumption that Retrieval and an isolated Personalized-Fit scorer must each satisfy every end-to-end quality expectation before Utility/Fusion may be built is not correct.

It is correct to require each layer to satisfy its own integrity, observability and minimum-input contracts. It is not correct to require:

- Retrieval to determine the final order of its Top 20;
- a Taste-derived Fit signal to improve the final ranking by itself;
- raw source scores, Context evidence or Personalization to act as a common utility scale before Fusion exists.

D4 already states that Retrieval optimizes recall rather than final rank and that Utility receives calibrated Intent, Context, observed Spot, Personalization and Confidence components. The later isolated experiments were scientifically valuable stress tests, but their results expose the missing integration boundary rather than invalidate the target architecture.

This is not gate manipulation. Wave 2.x and Wave 3C.1 remain failed experiments under their frozen contracts. D4.2 authorizes the missing architectural layer; it does not re-grade those experiments.

## 3. Retrieval Responsibility

Retrieval must:

- operate only over Product-, Distribution- and User-Hard-eligible Spots;
- find a broad, bounded and diagnostically attributable set of plausible Candidates;
- preserve Source, projection, rank, calibrated source evidence and Spot-data availability;
- expose misses, truncation, source contribution, latency, cost and uncertainty;
- provide enough relevant Candidates to permit a meaningful conditional ranker evaluation.

Retrieval must not:

- decide final Product utility;
- encode User Taste, Context, diversity and source quality into one opaque winning score;
- make a high source score equivalent to high Decision quality;
- compensate for missing final Fusion by over-optimizing source ordering against end-to-end utility;
- bypass Eligibility.

### Retrieval sufficiency for Wave 4

The current evidence is **sufficient for Wave-4 development and conditional evaluation**, not for Retrieval promotion or Product rollout.

Full-Pool Recall near `0.643` and Best Available near `0.691` leave a large measurable subset in which useful Candidates exist and can be reranked. Wave 2.3 also produced a large robust lift with complete source evidence and zero integrity leakage. That is enough to develop Utility scientifically if every result is partitioned into:

1. `RETRIEVAL_MISS` — the required Candidate was not supplied;
2. `UTILITY/FUSION_MISS` — a suitable supplied Candidate was ordered incorrectly;
3. `NO_EVALUABLE_RELEVANT_CANDIDATE` — conditional ranking is not scored as a success or failure.

The D4.1 Retrieval promotion contract remains an independent required gate before any end-to-end Product promotion. Wave 4 may not hide its inherited Retrieval ceiling with aggregate ranking metrics.

## 4. User Taste Engine Responsibility

The User Taste Engine must:

- learn signed, confidence-bearing Global, Place-Type and Contextual preferences from legitimate observed evidence;
- distinguish unknown, weak, contradictory, stale and reliable preferences;
- preserve provenance, Consent, recency, decay, drift and auditability;
- produce a current Taste projection without receiving Latent Truth;
- preserve explicit current Intent authority.

It must not:

- select eligible Candidates;
- decide the relative importance of request relevance, current Context, Spot evidence and Taste;
- turn Confidence into an unbounded ranking boost;
- produce final Decision utility or a final slate.

Wave 3B.1 proves that this foundation is sufficient for Wave 4. Its frozen Engine and treatment identities remain protected inputs.

## 5. Personalized Fit Responsibility

Personalized Fit should express bounded candidate-level evidence of:

> how compatible this eligible Candidate appears with the validated User preference state in the current relevant scope.

It must preserve signed direction, Confidence, provenance, scope and unknown state. It should not be expected to serve as an independent ranking engine or to improve end-to-end ranking when applied as an isolated additive replacement.

Wave 3C.1 proves that a standalone scalar Fit is insufficient: its lift is effectively zero, Context utility remains negative, and Mature benefit fails even though the underlying Taste Map is validated. The evidence supports architecture interpretation **B** from the mission: the primary unresolved issue is translating and balancing valid User evidence with Request, Context and Candidate evidence. That is a Utility/Fusion responsibility.

Wave 4 must therefore consume Personalized Fit as a decomposed evidence vector, not silently promote `backyrd-personalized-fit-v1.2` as a final score. Its observed components may be retained, recalibrated or rejected through pre-registered ablations. The failed Wave 3C.1 scalar remains the frozen control.

## 6. Utility Responsibility

Utility answers:

> Given an already eligible Candidate that Retrieval supplied, how valuable is it for this request, this moment and this User, with the evidence and uncertainty currently available?

The deterministic first Utility contract must combine separately traceable components for:

- explicit current Request/Intent fit;
- current Context fit;
- observed Spot evidence and data sufficiency;
- bounded Personalized Fit evidence;
- component Confidence and missing/unknown state;
- declared non-personalized quality or discovery evidence permitted by D4.

Utility must not contain hard requirements as weights. It must not use Latent Truth, Golden labels, future outcomes or Locked Holdout results as runtime inputs. It must not give Personalization influence merely because a User has more History.

## 7. Fusion Responsibility

Fusion must:

- place heterogeneous, calibrated evidence on an explicit comparable decision scale;
- resolve Request–Context–History conflicts according to the authority contract;
- limit uncertain or low-confidence evidence;
- preserve source and component ablations;
- combine evidence per canonical Candidate rather than choose a winning source object;
- produce a reproducible component ledger and uncertainty state.

Fusion must not directly add incomparable raw Semantic, lexical, structured or Taste scores. It cannot compensate for Eligibility failure, and it cannot claim recovery of Candidates Retrieval never supplied.

## 8. Final Ranking Responsibility

Final Ranking must:

- order the eligible supplied Candidate set by the fused Utility evidence;
- apply deterministic tie-breaking;
- expose conditional ranking quality separately from end-to-end quality;
- preserve the canonical Candidate identity and Distribution envelope;
- hand a reproducible order to later slate/diversity work.

Wave 4 may evaluate a bounded slate rule only to the extent already authorized by D4. It must not pull Wave-5 Remix, Explanation, Outcome Learning or Product rollout into scope.

## 9. Boundary Problems in the Previous Gates

The previous experiments remain valid, but three interpretations must be corrected prospectively:

1. **Retrieval Top-20 is not Final Top-20.** Capacity capture is a valid Retrieval constraint, but requiring source ordering to approximate final Decision utility caused Wave 2.4/2.5 to test work that belongs partly to conditional Utility.
2. **Personalized Fit is evidence, not a policy.** Requiring the isolated Fit term alone to produce robust final lift encouraged it to balance Context, Request relevance and Candidate quality without a Utility layer.
3. **Stage gates and program sequencing are different.** A failed standalone promotion gate prevents that component from being declared Product-ready; it does not always prevent building the downstream layer required to evaluate the component in its intended role.

No threshold is removed. The distinction is:

- component promotion remains blocked;
- controlled downstream architecture research is authorized;
- end-to-end promotion requires both component attribution and all applicable integrity/quality gates.

## 10. Utility Hypothesis

D4.2 accepts the following testable hypothesis:

```text
eligible candidate
  → calibrated request fit
  + calibrated context fit
  + bounded personalized evidence
  + observed spot/evidence quality
  + component confidence and uncertainty
  → deterministic hybrid utility
  → evidence fusion
  → conditional final ranking
```

This hypothesis directly addresses the observed boundary:

- Retrieval supplies materially better but incomplete opportunities;
- Taste accurately describes the User;
- isolated Fit cannot decide when that knowledge should outweigh other evidence;
- Utility/Fusion is the first layer whose explicit responsibility is that joint trade-off.

Authorization is falsifiable. If frozen-candidate conditional ranking does not improve, or if Personalization ablation provides no attributable value inside Utility, Wave 4 must fail rather than push more weight into Taste or disguise Retrieval misses.

## 12. Wave-4 Guardrails

Wave 4 is authorized only under these constraints:

1. Product, Distribution and User Hard Eligibility remain authoritative and precede Utility.
2. Explicit current Intent remains authoritative over History.
3. The validated Wave-3B.1 Taste Engine remains frozen unless a separate reviewed contract reopens it.
4. The Wave-3C.1 standalone Fit remains a frozen control, not a promoted score.
5. Retrieval is frozen for the main comparison; Retrieval changes require a separate experiment.
6. Retrieval misses, Utility/Fusion misses and Personalization misses remain distinct and mutually auditable.
7. Utility cannot force weak Personalization through a minimum weight or compensate a failed component gate with a composite score.
8. Confidence attenuates uncertain evidence; UNKNOWN is neutral, not negative.
9. No Latent Truth, evaluation Utility, Golden labels, future outcomes or Holdout-derived parameters may enter runtime features.
10. Development tuning, Regression validation and Locked Holdout remain separated. No threshold is selected from official results.
11. Every Candidate receives one canonical evidence envelope, including semantic-only and fallback Candidates.
12. The Flight Recorder exposes normalized inputs, component contributions, Confidence, conflicts, ablations, Utility, Fusion output, tie-break and final rank.
13. D0-F-002 remains a mandatory controlled fixture.
14. No Production connection, mutation, deployment or Product switch is authorized.

## 13. Wave-4 Measurement Model

Before implementation results are inspected, Wave 4 must freeze a versioned Validation Contract with at least four complementary views.

### A. Conditional ranking quality

Evaluate only Decisions whose frozen Candidate pool contains eligible Good-or-Better/Best-Available alternatives. Measure NDCG@10, Precision@10, regret, Top-K Utility, best-available rank, calibration and deterministic ties. This isolates Utility/Fusion from Retrieval.

### B. End-to-end quality

Run the unchanged candidate path and report total Decision quality alongside Retrieval coverage. End-to-end gains never erase D4.1 failures.

### C. Component and causal attribution

Use pre-registered ablations:

- Request only;
- Request + Context;
- Request + Context + observed Spot evidence;
- ACTUAL / NEUTRAL / OPPOSING Personalization under D2.2;
- Confidence enabled/disabled only as an approved diagnostic ablation;
- individual retrieval-source evidence removed without changing the Candidate set.

This must show whether User Taste adds value inside Utility rather than merely changes scores.

### D. Cohort and failure decomposition

Report Cold, Onboarding, Early/Sparse, Developing, Mature and Power users; Context, Place Type, category, data density, seed and split. Classify every material miss as Retrieval, Request/Context utility, Personalization, Spot-data insufficiency, Fusion/calibration, tie/slate or unknown.

The official Wave-4 questions are:

1. Does Utility/Fusion improve overall Decision quality?
2. Does it rank supplied good Candidates better under frozen-candidate evaluation?
3. Does ACTUAL History create positive attributable lift over NEUTRAL inside Utility?
4. Does the same User receive objectively better results in different Contexts?
5. Do different Users receive justified, beneficial differences for the same request?
6. Does current Intent remain authoritative under ACTUAL and OPPOSING History?
7. What share of remaining error is Retrieval?
8. What share is Utility/Fusion?
9. What share is Personalization?
10. What statistically supported improvement exists over frozen Legacy and current Wave controls?

Exact Wave-4 thresholds must be pre-registered from the existing Constitution/D4 objectives and statistical design before the official run. They may not be chosen from Wave-4 results. Existing D4 hard gates and the published `NDCG@10 >= 0.55` and `Precision@10 >= 0.35` floors remain applicable unless a separate scientific contract proves them invalid prospectively without historical re-grading.

## 14. Architecture Decision

**Decision A — WAVE 4 AUTHORIZED.**

The upstream layers are sufficient for controlled Utility/Fusion development:

- Eligibility is proven and authoritative;
- Retrieval provides a materially improved, observable Candidate pool large enough for conditional ranking research, while its known misses remain separately measurable;
- the User Taste Engine is validated;
- Personalized Fit is useful as bounded evidence but insufficient as a standalone ranker;
- the missing responsibility is the calibrated joint evaluation of Request, Context, User and Spot evidence.

This authorization does not permit Production rollout and does not promote Retrieval or Personalized Fit. Wave 4 itself must pass its frozen conditional, causal, cohort, integrity and end-to-end contracts before any later authorization.
