The mature population is intentionally not “mature” on only one trait: every mature/long-term profile passes the frozen richness floor of four global, two place-type and three contextual concepts, one negative-evidence concept and one known pattern. The Cold card stays genuinely empty: a neutral current request does not become a preference.

## Lifecycle behavior

For every non-cold profile, an onboarding event is followed by early and developing prefixes before the final history rebuild. The N2 state moves from `EARLY` through `DEVELOPING` to `LONG_TERM` once independent-session evidence is sufficient. The developing card remains `DEVELOPING` after nine sessions; the cold card remains `COLD`.

## Relevant projection examples

`NORTH_STAR_EXPLORER_01` has low sufficiency for `FAMILY_SUNDAY` (one global cozy signal only) but high sufficiency for Friends Friday, Date Evening, Solo Afterwork and the Copenhagen arrival moment. That is desired: long history is not a licence to pretend contextual knowledge exists. The Family planner exposes a recurring Family Sunday pattern; the Social/nightlife card exposes a Friends late-night pattern. Copenhagen uses the same portable user state and local current moment, not local visit history.
