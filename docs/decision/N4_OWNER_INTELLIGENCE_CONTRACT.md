# N4 Owner Intelligence Contract v1

## Non-negotiable boundary

**PAY TO BE UNDERSTOOD — NOT PAY TO RANK.**

Owner Premium can never buy ranking position, Utility or Personalization bonus, Distribution priority, AI preference or guaranteed organic impressions. The N4 snapshot and N6 serialization prohibit payment/tier fields as Decision inputs.

## Free and Premium

All Spots, including Free Spots, receive the best profile Backyrd can derive from legitimate canonical, community and Outcome evidence. Free Owners can maintain core Facts. Premium Owners may additionally submit bounded structured interpretation claims across the shared Spot Concept space.

Premium entitlement is resolved server-side from a service-controlled entitlement row. A client-provided Premium flag has no authority. Billing itself is not implemented in N4 because the repository has no canonical Premium entitlement source yet.

## Claims are Evidence

An Owner claim enters `PENDING_EVIDENCE` or `NEEDS_REVIEW`; it is never canonical truth by submission alone. Canonical snapshots are service-derived and can be supported or contradicted by Backyrd, community and Outcome evidence. Owner verification modestly changes source reliability but does not dominate independent Outcomes.

## Anti-gaming

The contract enforces bounded values, schema allowlists, tier fields, stable idempotency, rate limiting, contradictory-pair detection, provenance and audit history. Claims such as quiet+lively or budget+premium in the same Context are retained as contradictions and cannot maximize fit for everything.

## Security and privacy

Owners may submit/read claims only for their own Spot. Direct client writes to entitlement, canonical evidence, snapshots and audit data are denied. Derived evidence is service-only. Owner-facing data never exposes User IDs, private Taste, individual Decision History, private Outcomes or Trust/moderation evidence.
