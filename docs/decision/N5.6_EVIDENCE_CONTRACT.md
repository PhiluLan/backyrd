## Fact/inference boundary

N2 Memory records facts with identity, time, provenance, consent, decision/session/spot references and minimized moment signature. N5.6 Evidence Chains are versioned interpretations. Learned nodes are rebuildable inference, never rewritten historical fact.

## Journey chain

The stable journey key is `userId + sessionId + decisionId + spotId`. For each concept and scope only the strongest qualified disposition contributes. Duplicate event IDs are idempotent; conflicting reuse fails. Repeated same-spot activity does not become independent evidence.

## Evidence hierarchy

Exposure and result impression are neutral. Opens/taps are weak. Save/navigation/reservation express deliberate intent. Verified visit expresses a visit, not satisfaction. Explicit post-visit feedback and negative feedback are outcome-near evidence. `not_there` is a correction, not dislike. Current requests do not write taste.

Base strengths come unchanged from the frozen Wave-3B.1 Evidence Model. N5.6 applies separately versioned reliability, N4 attribution confidence, bounded concept dilution, independence and recent/long-term decay. This is a new inference identity; it does not mutate the historical Taste Engine freeze.

## Concept attribution

Learning is allowed only for canonical Taste concepts actually attributed to the spot. Attribution below 0.35 is UNKNOWN and contributes nothing. At most eight concepts per event are admitted, with `1/sqrt(n)` dilution. No raw description or evaluator truth becomes user evidence.

## Exposure bias

The model does not learn negative preference from non-selection or missing activity. It keeps selection and satisfaction distinct. The Lab world lacks propensity-corrected natural exposures; production propensity adjustment remains an explicit limitation and future acceptance requirement.

## Privacy and deletion

Consent purpose is `personalized_recommendations`. Missing consent fails closed. Withdrawal or deletion requires removal of eligible source events followed by deterministic rebuild; derived preferences may not survive without their evidence.
