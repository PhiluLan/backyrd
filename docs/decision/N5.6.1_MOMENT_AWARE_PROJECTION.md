## Root cause summary

The N5.6 projection treated overall evidence volume as decision-specific knowledge. Strong time, place-type, and global nodes repeatedly filled an eight-node output even when the explicit social context had no matching history. `solo_friendly` therefore appeared outside Solo, Family and Broad moments inherited `HIGH`, and the cap behaved like a target.

N5.6.1 separates:

1. overall User maturity;
2. Moment clarity;
3. applicable Context knowledge;
4. applicable Place-Type knowledge;
5. portable Global fallback;
6. matching Occasion support;
7. final decision-specific personalization sufficiency.

## Architecture

```text
FULL SIGNED USER CARD
        +
N3 CURRENT MOMENT
        ↓
CONCEPT / SCOPE COMPATIBILITY
        ↓
INDEPENDENT RELEVANCE FILTER
        ↓
REDUNDANCY + POLARITY CONTRAST
        ↓
MAXIMUM CAP (never a fill target)
        ↓
MINIMUM SUFFICIENT PROJECTION
        +
DECISION-SPECIFIC SUFFICIENCY
```

Every User-Card node is considered exactly once and receives `SELECTED` or a canonical suppression reason. The runtime path does not use scenario IDs, persona labels, evaluator data, latent truth, Candidates, or ranking.

## Moment clarity

Clarity is bounded and derived from explicit social context, concrete activity/domain, explicit vibe, occasion, budget, planning tolerance, distance, duration, spontaneity, and environment. Time and city remain useful facts but cannot make a vague desire clear by themselves.

`BROAD_UNKNOWN` is therefore low clarity even for a DEEP User. `SOLO_AFTERWORK` is clear because social context, domain, occasion, planning, and distance are explicit.

## Compatibility and portability

All frozen Taste concepts receive versioned metadata:

- broadly portable;
- conditionally portable;
- Context/social bound;
- Place-Type bound;
- time bound where applicable.

`social_style.solo_friendly` is governed by generic social-context metadata (`solo`), not a scenario-specific exception. A high affinity cannot override incompatible Moment semantics.

## Relevance and fallback

The relevance function is bounded and explainable. It combines compatibility, semantic relevance, scope specificity, confidence, independent evidence quality, and explicit penalties for broad fallback and contradictions. Thresholds are `0.64` for sufficiently clear moments and `0.72` for broad/low-clarity moments.

Fallback levels:

- `0`: matching Context;
- `1`: matching Place Type;
- `2`: portable Global fallback;
- `3`: no applicable evidence / UNKNOWN.

Global evidence must be semantically portable and relevant. `GLOBAL` no longer means universally applicable.

## Projection budget and redundancy

Eight is a maximum. It is never a target. A node must independently pass relevance before capacity is considered. Same-family, same-polarity nodes are reduced to the stronger signal; an opposite-polarity node may remain when it adds contrastive value.

## Current Intent and signal type

Current Intent remains authoritative. Conflicting history is suppressed as `CURRENT_INTENT_CONFLICT`.

Selected evidence is classified as:

- `CORROBORATIVE`: independently supported history confirms current explicit intent;
- `INDEPENDENT_PERSONALIZATION_SIGNAL`: adds User knowledge beyond current intent.

Unsubstantiated duplication is suppressed as `REDUNDANT_WITH_CURRENT_INTENT`. Independent-signal rate is `0.852761`.

## Sufficiency

Sufficiency is not an average and is not inherited from maturity. It exposes overall maturity, Moment clarity, matching social Context knowledge, temporal support, Place-Type knowledge, Global fallback strength, Occasion support, evidence depth, contradictions, fallback depth, projection confidence, and signed breadth.

DEVELOPING, EARLY, and COLD states have conservative maximums. An explicit social context without matching social-context history is penalized even when Place-Type or time history is strong.
