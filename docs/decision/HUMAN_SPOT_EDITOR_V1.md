# Human Spot Editor V1

The Human Spot Editor is the primary authoring surface for trustworthy Spot understanding. Admins, Founders and Owners answer normal Product questions; canonical keys and derived intelligence remain behind the server boundary.

## Product model

```text
Human answer
  -> scoped source-bound proposal
  -> Founder/Admin review where required
  -> accepted typed fact
  -> frozen qualification mapper
  -> atomic N4 snapshot + human readiness
  -> Decision package and authorized factual reasons
```

The browser cannot write accepted facts, N4 evidence, confidence, snapshots, readiness or ranking inputs. Accepted history is superseded, never overwritten. Founder/Admin may explicitly retract or stale a problematic accepted fact; the correction, audit record and derived rebuild share one transaction.

## Human sections

- Basic Product information remains the familiar name, category, description, location, contact, media and opening-hours editor.
- “Was kann man hier machen?” uses the controlled activity catalog.
- Suitability asks about indoor/outdoor, rain, families, age, supervision, reservation and visit duration.
- Audience and social context cover Solo, Date, Friends, Family, Groups and Work.
- Deep fields cover atmosphere, noise, conversation, accessibility, daypart and distinctive characteristics.

Every unknown answer may remain unknown. Optional unknown fields do not block Gold. Missing core identity, invalid canonical identity, stale material truth and unresolved material conflicts do.

Legacy free-text intelligence is collapsed and labelled as non-canonical. It remains only for operational compatibility and cannot qualify Gold truth.

## Roles

| Actor | Scope | Authoring |
|---|---|---|
| Founder/Admin | every Spot | Basic + Deep, review, retract/stale, read-only derived diagnosis |
| Owner Basic | owned Spots | standard profile, activities, environment, basic family/audience, price and planning |
| Owner Pro | owned Spots | Basic plus age, rain, detailed social suitability, atmosphere, noise, conversation, accessibility, daypart and signature details |

Entitlement controls authoring only. Downgrade retains accepted Deep truth. Subscription tier is absent from N4 and organic Decision inputs.

## Sources and scope

The UI asks “Woher stammt diese Information?” and whether it applies generally, only to an event/program, or temporarily. Only `SPOT` scope may enter general accepted Spot truth. Event/program/temporary facts remain auditable and cannot qualify general N4.

Official sources require a URL. Admin-verified knowledge and Owner claims require a stable reference. Owners cannot self-accept restricted truth.

## One readiness contract

`backyrd-human-spot-readiness-v1` is the authoritative Product readiness. It reports concrete human gaps such as description, images, contact, opening hours, environment or age—never `BASIC_FACTS` or N4 completeness. Accepted website/phone facts are projected consistently into Product basis data, so no duplicate entry is needed.

The category adapter is the only place-type authority. For example, `Museum` becomes `culture`; an invalid historical `MUSEUM` value is surfaced for review and cannot serialize as a FULL Decision candidate.

## Rollout

The additive migration enables the internal Admin/Founder editor. Public Owner V2 remains disabled by the existing server switch and client flag. No frozen Taste/N4 registry or Decision ranking formula is changed.
