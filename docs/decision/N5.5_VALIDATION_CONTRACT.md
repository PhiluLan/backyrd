# N5.5 — Validation Contract

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE MEASUREMENT**

The versioned contract is [`decision-lab/config/n5-5-validation-contract-v1.json`](../../decision-lab/config/n5-5-validation-contract-v1.json). It fixes the population and maturity floors before results are inspected:

- at least 9 users;
- mature users: at least 100 events, 40 sessions, 25 spots and 180 history days where applicable;
- at least 30 N5 decision projections;
- canonical-memory validity and evaluator/runtime isolation;
- profile richness and lifecycle progression;
- same-user/different-moment and different-user/same-moment differentiation;
- cross-city portability and current-intent authority;
- N5 projection bounds and privacy boundary;
- at least 50% medium-or-high diagnostic personalization opportunity.

Each binary integrity gate has threshold `1`; opportunity coverage has threshold `0.5`. Thresholds were not changed after the run. No composite can compensate for a failed integrity gate.

The official run produced 54 projections: 9 users × 6 current moments. It is deterministic and uses no AI transport. The sealed result is [`decision-lab/baselines/n5-5-longitudinal-user-intelligence-world-v1.json`](../../decision-lab/baselines/n5-5-longitudinal-user-intelligence-world-v1.json).
