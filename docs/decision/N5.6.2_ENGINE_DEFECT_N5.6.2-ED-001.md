# N5.6.2-ED-001 — Explicit Negative Outcome Lost After Verified Visit

Classification: **ENGINE DEFECT**

Affected component: frozen N5.6 Evidence Chain, strongest-disposition selection per Journey × Concept × Scope.

## Reproduction

One legitimate Product journey contains:

1. Spot open
2. navigation intent
3. verified visit
4. explicit negative post-visit feedback

For each shared Spot Concept, N5.6 retains the highest magnitude sample. The positive visit has weighted magnitude `0.3936 × Spot confidence × dilution`; the negative post-visit event has `0.3496 × Spot confidence × dilution`. The negative sample is therefore dropped.

Across seeds 56201/56202/56203, all `28/57/54` explicit-negative journeys lost every negative sample. Final User Cards contained zero negative nodes.

## Why this is not a World defect

A visit followed by “not my thing” is observable, internally consistent Product behavior. `verified_visit` proves the visit, while explicit feedback communicates satisfaction. The latter must not be erased by the former.

## Required future work

No fix is authorized in N5.6.2. A separate Engine change must define semantic precedence or multi-signal preservation within a Journey and prove:

- explicit negative outcome survives a verified visit;
- visit remains useful without being treated as satisfaction;
- positive outcomes, negative outcomes and corrections retain distinct meaning;
- Wave-3B.1, N5.6 and N5.6.1 contracts do not regress;
- historical verdicts remain unchanged.
