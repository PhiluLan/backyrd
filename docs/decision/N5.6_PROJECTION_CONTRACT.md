# N5.6 Signed Relevant Projection Contract

Version: `backyrd-n5-6-signed-projection-v1`

N5.6 receives a Full User Card plus N3 Current Moment and explicit intent. It returns a candidate-independent, signed, bounded projection. It does not rank and does not call an LLM.

## Hierarchy and authority

Matching context replaces matching place-type, which replaces global fallback for the same concept. Explicit current intent and N3 Current Moment are authoritative. A historical preference that conflicts with current intent is suppressed; one that merely duplicates explicit intent is also suppressed to avoid double counting.

## Payload groups

- at most 6 positive taste nodes;
- at most 3 negative taste nodes;
- at most 2 behavioral preferences;
- at most 2 occurrence patterns;
- up to 40 prioritized suppression-audit entries.

Every taste item carries concept, signed affinity, polarity, confidence, relevance, source layer/scope, trend, evidence depth, evidence references and reason codes. Patterns require a matching current context. Empty/low-evidence states remain valid and produce uncertainty codes.

## Suppression

Reasons include current-intent override, duplicate explicit intent, wrong-context pattern, less-specific duplicate scope, low confidence, current irrelevance, and positive/negative budget. The audit distinguishes “not known” from “known but not relevant now.”

## Frozen limitation

The official North Star projections expose a product-level concern: broad global fallback still makes several moments overly similar and gives Family/Broad moments HIGH sufficiency. The structural contract passes, but human relevance is marked NEEDS WORK. No post-freeze threshold or rule was changed.
