# N5.6.2 Realistic Longitudinal User World

Status: **STOPPED — ENGINE DEFECT N5.6.2-ED-001**

N5.6.2 was designed as a world-only treatment on the frozen N2, Wave-3B.1, N5.6 and N5.6.1 stack. Development preflight produced a deterministic 12-user, three-seed longitudinal population, then stopped before the official world freeze because the realistic event chains exposed a material defect in the frozen N5.6 Evidence Chain.

- N5.6 historical verdict remains **FAIL**, including negative-preference accuracy `0.888889`.
- N5.6.1 remains **PASS**.
- Historical N6A remains **FAIL**.

## World architecture

The treatment separates four identities:

1. World: persistent people, usage cadence, Spots, sessions and observable events.
2. Engine: frozen N2/N5.6 Evidence Chain and User Card.
3. Projection: frozen N5.6.1 relevance and sufficiency.
4. Evaluator: independent latent/action generator plus observability-bounded Learnable Truth.

Only canonical Product-like events cross the World → Engine boundary. Evaluator-only tendencies, hidden outcomes, opportunity sets and persona labels do not enter Memory, User Intelligence or projection.

## Preflight population

Each of the three deterministic seeds generated:

- 12 stable users: eight long-term, two developing and two cold/light controls;
- 768 sessions over 1–36 months;
- 5,168–5,201 canonical events;
- 88 N4-compatible Spots across eight Place Types and four cities;
- Decision, direct-map, search/reservation, ignored-result, repeat-favorite and shared-link sessions;
- travel, noise, group compromise, logistics-driven choice, missing telemetry and incomplete outcomes;
- probabilistic positive, neutral, negative and unknown outcomes;
- explicit feedback in only 10.4–13.3% of sessions;
- missing outcomes in 58.6–60.4% of sessions.

`NORTH_STAR_REALISTIC_01` spans approximately three years and has 795 Product events in seed 56201. Final learned values were not hardcoded.

## Stop finding

A canonical Journey can contain:

`verified_visit → negative_post_visit`

N5.6 keeps only the largest weighted sample for the same Journey × Concept × Scope. Before Spot confidence and concept dilution:

- `verified_visit`: `0.48 × 0.82 = 0.3936` positive
- `negative_post_visit`: `0.38 × 0.92 = 0.3496` negative
- `explicit_negative`: `0.22 × 0.92 = 0.2024` negative

The visit therefore wins and the later negative outcome is discarded. Across the three preflight seeds:

| Seed | Explicit-negative journeys | Negative samples retained | Learned negative nodes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 56201 | 28 | 0 | 0 |
| 56202 | 57 | 0 | 0 |
| 56203 | 54 | 0 | 0 |

This is not a generator anomaly: a visit followed by negative feedback is legitimate Product-observable behavior. A visit confirms presence, not satisfaction. It must not erase the explicit post-visit outcome.
