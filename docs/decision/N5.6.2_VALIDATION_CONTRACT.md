## Stop precedence

Stop conditions override metric aggregation. Confirmed Engine defects, latent leakage, invalid Product events, evaluator circularity, rebuild divergence or post-freeze threshold changes prohibit an official result.

- no mandatory gate matrix was promoted;
- no diagnostic metric is an official N5.6.2 verdict;
- thresholds were not changed after the finding;
- the Engine was not repaired inside this World treatment.

An official rerun requires a separately versioned Engine fix followed by a fresh World/Evaluator freeze.
