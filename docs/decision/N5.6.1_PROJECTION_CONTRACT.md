# N5.6.1 Projection Contract

Version: `backyrd-n5-6-1-moment-aware-projection-v1`

## Purpose

Return the smallest evidence-backed subset of the signed N5.6 User Card that materially helps the current Decision. The contract is candidate-independent and performs no ranking.

## Pipeline

1. derive Moment clarity and canonical Context/Place-Type keys;
2. assess every User-Card node against versioned Concept metadata;
3. fail closed on Context, Place-Type, or Current-Intent conflict;
4. require minimum confidence `0.22` and signed polarity;
5. require relevance `0.64`, or `0.72` for broad/low-clarity Moments;
6. prefer matching audience Context, then Place Type, time support, then portable Global evidence;
7. remove weaker duplicate scopes and same-family/same-polarity redundancy;
8. apply the total maximum of eight only after relevance filtering;
9. persist selected and suppressed dispositions.

## Selected-node fields

Each selected node includes Concept, signed affinity/polarity, source scope, confidence, decision relevance, Moment compatibility, fallback level, trend, evidence depth/references, contradiction state, signal type, and reason codes.

Reason codes include `MATCHING_CONTEXT_TASTE`, `MATCHING_PLACE_TYPE_TASTE`, `PORTABLE_GLOBAL_TASTE`, `CURRENT_INTENT_CORROBORATION`, `INDEPENDENT_USER_SIGNAL`, and `RELEVANT_NEGATIVE_AVOIDANCE`.

## Suppression

Canonical reasons include:

- `CONTEXT_MISMATCH`
- `PLACE_TYPE_MISMATCH`
- `LOW_RELEVANCE`
- `LOW_CONFIDENCE`
- `CURRENT_INTENT_CONFLICT`
- `REDUNDANT_WITH_CURRENT_INTENT`
- `REDUNDANT_WITH_STRONGER_NODE`
- `OUTSIDE_PROJECTION_CAP`
- `GLOBAL_FALLBACK_NOT_JUSTIFIED`
- `INSUFFICIENT_SUPPORT`
- `CONTEXT_UNKNOWN_FOR_CONTEXT_SENSITIVE_CONCEPT`

Unsupported or unknown knowledge is not silently omitted.

## Authority and boundaries

Explicit Current Intent > N3 Current Moment > N5.6.1 projection > long-term history. The output contains no raw history, Candidate data, Latent Truth, private Trust data, ranking, or N6 decision.
