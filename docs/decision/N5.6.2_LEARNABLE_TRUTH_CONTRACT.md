# N5.6.2 Learnable Truth Contract

Version: `backyrd-n5-6-2-learnable-truth-v1`

Learnable Truth is independent of the Engine output. It answers what direction is epistemically supportable from Product-observable history, not what the hidden synthetic person tends to prefer.

## Inputs

- observable explicit feedback;
- bounded commitment/intent when feedback is missing;
- canonical Spot Intelligence and confidence;
- independent sessions and Spots;
- known Context and Place Type;
- event time and provenance.

Hidden synthetic satisfaction affects generated behavior but is available to Learnable Truth only when an explicit feedback event was emitted. A verified visit without feedback is not satisfaction.

## Boundaries

- Concept attribution requires Spot confidence `>= 0.35` and is diluted by observable Concept count.
- Durable expectations require independent sessions, independent Spots and observable outcome support.
- A durable negative requires at least two independent negative sessions and Spots plus sufficient negative support.
- A single weak or hidden negative remains `UNKNOWN`.
- Evaluator rules do not import N5.6 confidence or affinity thresholds.

The preflight proved this distinction was necessary: the realistic world contained valid learnable negative journeys, while the frozen Engine removed their negative samples before aggregation.
