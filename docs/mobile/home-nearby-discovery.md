Task class: PRODUCT_RELEASE for the Home continuation of installed Decision results and account-bound local cache. No schema, RLS, Decision ranking or dependency changes.

With an existing location consent and OS permission, Home obtains a fresh position without prompting. It loads approved places in complete spherical bounding boxes covering 15 km, irrespective of profile city or city borders. Stable UUID pagination prevents the former newest-160/newest-14 truncation from discarding closer places. Invalid/missing coordinates and places outside the actual Haversine circle are omitted; canonical Distribution eligibility is applied before selecting the nearest results. Distribution visibility is checked in batches of 500, below the public RPC row cap, so a large local catalog cannot silently lose nearer eligible places. Distance is primary, with stable ID ties. Up to six cards are shown, including clearly marked closed places. Distances are air-line distances, never walking-route claims.

Bounding boxes handle the date line and poles. Retrieval has a ten-second deadline and a 10,000-row completeness ceiling; an incomplete catalog fails honestly instead of presenting an arbitrary subset as the nearest. Only the selected places load canonical cover images. Existing canonical hours feed the status badge.

No consent, denied OS permission, unavailable/invalid coordinates or a last-known location produces a clearly labelled profile-city catalog, without distance or a nearby claim. Nearby retrieval failure displays an error, not a silently substituted city list. Focus, return to foreground and pull-to-refresh repeat the location/consent check. Request-generation guards prevent earlier location/data responses from replacing a newer result. Coordinates stay in screen memory and are not added to analytics/logs. “Alle ansehen” opens the existing map at the current point; city fallback opens the existing city list.

“Neu auf Backyrd” uses the same complete local catalog as nearby discovery, sorted strictly by `spots.created_at` descending with stable ID ties. It displays six eligible places, independent of Distribution priority and profile edits. With current location the scope is the same 15 km circle; without it a complete, case-insensitive profile-city catalog is paginated and visibility-checked before selecting the newest six. Missing coordinates are allowed for the city fallback. `created_at` denotes entry on Backyrd, not a venue opening date. Home Moments retain their existing city scope.

All three Home place rails (discovery, new, last Decision search) use `HomeSpotRail`, including canonical photos, Mood signature, opening status, typography and snap geometry. The last successful Decision search appears directly below new places with the submitted wish as its heading and the originally presented place IDs in engine order. Event-only candidates are not converted into place claims. A successful empty search replaces the previous results; a failed request does not invent or re-run a search. Current approved status, Distribution visibility, public facts and cover photos are reloaded before showing stored IDs. Removed/hidden places disappear and old Decision availability or explanations are not reused as current facts.

Only the latest query, up to 50 place IDs and timestamp are cached on this device (native SecureStore; tab session storage for browser preview). The cache is bound to the authenticated actor, expires after 30 days and is cleared on sign-out/account change. Writes are serialized and generation-guarded against late results and logout races. It is not analytics, a preference signal or cross-device search history. No GPS or recommendation reasons are persisted. Storage failure leaves only the current in-memory search usable. An account without a completed search has no last-search rail.
