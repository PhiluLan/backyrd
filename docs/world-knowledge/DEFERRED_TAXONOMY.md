# Deferred taxonomy inventory

Slice 1 does not canonicalize the 741 prototype definitions. Deferred areas include:

- full subcategory and place-type coverage across all 16 categories
- comprehensive cuisines, food specialities, products, services, activities, amenities, and constraints
- atmosphere, mood, romance, spontaneity, celebrations, and other subjective Fits
- complete age, alcohol, admission, weather, noise, area, booking, duration, and availability policies
- event entities and permanent/temporary Spot relationships
- source registries, moderation workflow, verification authorities, and conflict adjudication
- complete category Applicability rules
- owner authoring entitlements and subscription products
- AI extraction pipelines and provenance review
- multilingual aliases and taxonomy governance UI
- calibrated staleness policies by fact type
- Capability→Intent mappings, weights, thresholds, and ranking semantics
- Late-service derivation until Product defines the meaning of “late”

Deferred does not mean discarded. Each candidate must be reviewed for domain, data type, source authority, temporal behavior, and engine authorization before entering a later registry version.
